# Quest Curation Guidelines

Every quest in Kenya Quest is designed to offer an authentic, walkable or short-transit narrative experience through Nairobi.

## Curation Principles

1. **Five Distinct Checkpoints**: Each quest features exactly five curated stops sequenced in logical geographical order to minimize backtracking.
2. **Actionable Tasks**: Tasks should encourage sensory and cultural interaction (e.g., taste a specialty roast, photograph a monument, explore an artisanal workshop).
3. **Pacing and Accessibility**:
   - Total walking/transit duration should typically range between 1.5 to 3.5 hours.
   - Routes should consider pedestrian safety, opening hours, and daylight visibility.
4. **Local Partnership & Independent Spaces**: Prioritize independent Kenyan-owned establishments, cultural institutions, and public green spaces.
5. **Accurate Coordinates**: All latitude/longitude coordinates must correspond to the primary visitor entrance.
