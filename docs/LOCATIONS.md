# Nairobi Neighbourhoods Guide

Kenya Quest curates exploration across Nairobi's distinct districts. Each area offers a unique cultural and spatial rhythm.

## Covered Districts

### 1. Central Business District (CBD)
- **Vibe**: High density, historical civic monuments, bustling avenues, architectural heritage.
- **Key Landmarks**: Kenyatta International Convention Centre (KICC), City Hall, August 7th Memorial Park.
- **Characteristics**: Grid layout, rich modernist architecture, dense foot traffic.

### 2. Westlands
- **Vibe**: Vibrant culinary culture, creative studios, commercial hubs, and nightlife.
- **Key Landmarks**: Sarit Centre area, artisanal cafes, contemporary art galleries.
- **Characteristics**: Fast-paced, walkable pockets, fusion dining.

### 3. Kilimani & Hurlingham
- **Vibe**: Cosmopolitan residential, lush garden restaurants, independent coffee houses.
- **Key Landmarks**: Yaya Centre hub, boutique craft shops, tranquil avenues.
- **Characteristics**: Tree-lined side streets, relaxed daytime cafes.

### 4. Karen
- **Vibe**: Serene, expansive green tracts, historical estates, artisan craft markets.
- **Key Landmarks**: Karen Blixen Museum, Kazuri Beads, Oloolua Nature Trail.
- **Characteristics**: Semi-rural greenery, wildlife sanctuaries nearby, relaxed weekend pace.

### 5. Upper Hill
- **Vibe**: Elevated financial skyline, panoramic city viewpoints, contemporary towers.
- **Key Landmarks**: Modern architectural overlooks and terrace lounges.
- **Characteristics**: Modernist high-rises, expansive city views.
